Footy Ready Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Footy Ready is built to collect as little as possible. There is no account, no server, and no analytics in the App Store release. Here is exactly what that means.

What Footy Ready does not do

  • It does not require or create an account.
  • It does not send any data to a server — there is no server.
  • It does not use any analytics or advertising SDK.
  • It does not share, sell, or transmit any information to the developer or anyone else, except where you choose to during the TestFlight beta (see below).

What is stored, and where

Everything Footy Ready remembers — your training profile (birth year and month, equipment, goals, session length, any injuries you have flagged), your season dates, your daily readiness check-ins, and every session you log — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. None of it leaves your device on its own.

Under-18 athletes

Footy Ready asks for a birth year and month, not a full date of birth, and uses it only on-device to apply age-appropriate content rules — for example, keeping the training load conservative and never showing a weight-loss goal to an athlete under 18. This information is never uploaded anywhere.

The TestFlight beta

If you are testing Footy Ready through TestFlight, the app includes a tool to export your training data (profile, check-ins and logged sessions) as a JSON file, so you can email it to the developer if something looks wrong. This is entirely manual and opt-in — nothing is exported or sent unless you choose to, and this tool is not present in the App Store release.

Crash reports

Footy Ready does not include any crash-reporting SDK. If you have opted in to iOS's own "Share With App Developers" setting (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send the developer anonymised crash diagnostics through Xcode. This is Apple's own, user-consented mechanism, not something Footy Ready adds.

Purchases

Footy Ready's subscription is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The developer never receives or has access to your payment details — that relationship is between you and Apple.

Health disclaimer

Footy Ready gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice, and is not physiotherapy or rehabilitation. While an area is flagged as sore, the app leaves its work out of your plan rather than training around it — see a health professional about anything more than that.

Children's privacy

Footy Ready carries a 13+ age rating and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that shares data differently — this page will be updated and the date above will change.
