Support

Need help with Footy Ready? Email mattsappdevelopment@gmail.com and you'll get a reply directly from the developer. This is a one-person app, not a support team.

Common questions

Why did my session change from what was planned?

Your daily readiness check-in (sleep, energy, soreness, stress, mood) can pull a session back or swap it for a recovery day. The reason is always shown on the session itself: look for the "why" line.

How the check-in changes a session

How do I change which days I train this week?

On the Season tab, tap Change this week and set any day to Train, Team training or Off. The week is built around what you choose. Your usual week is in Settings → Your week. If you train with a club, a card on Today also asks each week whether your team is training, and it opens the week so you can mark the nights.

Where do I log a run, a game or training that isn't in my plan?

Tap Log something extra on Today or Progress. It takes a run, a gym session, a game, team training or another sport, and the entry shows on Progress and on the Season tab. A game, anything hard, or an hour or more eases your session the day after.

I forgot to log a session earlier this week.

Open the Season tab and tap Log it on that day, or use the line on Today, and it goes on the day you did it. This works for the current week only. A session you did early lands on the day you did it too, and the rest of the week moves to keep hard days apart.

I'm going away. What happens to my plan?

Add the dates under Settings → Season → Time away, and those days read Away. Morning prompts pause while you're away, and they stop if the app stays closed for two weeks. When you come back, Welcome back asks whether you kept training, and the phase you return to starts its climb again.

Can I change my equipment, session length or days without starting again?

Yes. Equipment, session length, running, goals and your days are all in Settings, and your history stays. If you've already logged a session this week, the change waits until Monday. You can add an injury there too, under Injuries → Add an injury.

Is this medical or physiotherapy advice?

No. Footy Ready gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice, and is not physiotherapy or rehabilitation. If something is more than mild soreness, see a health professional.

How do I send you my data if something looks wrong?

Open Settings → Export your data → Prepare your data export, then share the file straight to Mail. That sends your profile, season dates, check-ins and logged sessions as one JSON file. Nothing is sent unless you do this yourself.

How do I cancel or check my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple, not the app. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. A lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel and never renews.

I've paid or changed phones and it's not showing up.

In Footy Ready, open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. That brings back a subscription or a lifetime purchase made on the same Apple Account, and nothing is charged again. If you're on the paywall screen, Restore Subscription there brings back a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple, not the developer. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
